Privacy Policy

App: PixOrg  |  Package: com.pixorg.pixorg  |  Last updated: 6 Sep 2026

PixOrg ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private photo gallery for Android that lets you find any photo by the text or the scene in it. All recognition and search happens on your phone. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

The short version: your photos, the text recognised in them, and the AI "fingerprints" used for search never leave your device. We run no servers, have no user accounts, and include no analytics or advertising SDKs. We cannot see your photos even if we wanted to.

1. Data We Access on Your Device

  • Photos and videos: With your permission (Photos and videos access), the app reads the images stored on your phone to display them as a gallery. You can grant access to all photos or only to selected ones; the app works with whatever you choose.
  • Text recognised in photos (OCR): The app runs on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit, offline model) so you can search for words that appear in receipts, whiteboards, screenshots and documents. The recognised text is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and nowhere else.
  • AI image fingerprints (embeddings): A small vision model (MobileCLIP, run with ONNX Runtime) turns each photo into a list of numbers that describes what the picture looks like. These numbers power "looks like" search. They are computed and stored on your device only and cannot be turned back into the original photo.
  • Photo location (EXIF GPS): If a photo already contains location data recorded by your camera, the app reads it to place the photo on the Map view. The app never uses your phone's GPS and never records where you are.
  • Ratings, tags and albums: Star ratings, tags and smart-album rules you create are stored in the app's local database. If you enable XMP sync, ratings and tags are also written into the photo file itself (or a sidecar file next to it) so other photo apps can read them. Each write to a file you own is confirmed through the Android system dialog.
  • Receipts export: When you export receipts as a CSV file, the file is handed to the Android share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice.
  • Vault: Photos you move into the Vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key stored in your phone's secure keystore, and are unlocked with your device biometrics or screen-lock credential. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data; Android handles that check and only tells the app whether it succeeded.

2. Data That Leaves Your Device

No photo, recognised text, AI fingerprint, tag, rating or location is ever transmitted to us or to anyone else. The app connects to the internet for only two purposes:

PurposeWho receives what
Map tiles (only when you open the Map view) Map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers. As with any web request, the tile server sees your IP address and which map areas you view. It does not receive your photos or their coordinates individually. Subject to the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
Purchases (only if you buy PixOrg Pro) Payments are processed by Google Play Billing, and subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. See Section 4.

Everything else, including the AI models, is shipped inside the app and runs fully offline. You can use every feature except the Map view and purchases with the internet switched off.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

PixOrg contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not receive usage statistics, device identifiers, or crash reports from the app. If you choose to share a crash report through Google Play, that report is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

PixOrg is free to use, with an optional PixOrg Pro upgrade sold as an in-app purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. Subscription entitlements are checked through RevenueCat, which receives a random, app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt from Google Play in order to confirm that your subscription is active. RevenueCat does not receive your photos, your name, or your email address. RevenueCat's handling of purchase data is described in its Privacy Policy; Google Play's is in Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, upload or back up your photos or videos.
  • We do not collect the text recognised in your photos or the AI fingerprints derived from them.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, camera feed, or biometric data.
  • We do not require or create a user account, and we do not collect your name or email.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your photos or any derived data to train AI models. The models in the app are fixed and never learn from your library.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything the app creates (the search index, recognised text, AI fingerprints, ratings, tags, smart albums, receipts data and the Vault) lives in the app's private storage on your phone and remains entirely under your control. To delete it:

  • Search index: Settings → AI index → Re-index rebuilds it from scratch.
  • Vault: restore photos to your gallery from the Vault screen before uninstalling, otherwise they are deleted with the app.
  • Everything: Android Settings → Apps → PixOrg → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes the index, the Vault and its encryption keys.

Ratings and tags that you chose to write into photo files with XMP sync stay in those files, because they belong to you; you can clear them in the app before turning XMP sync off. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children's Privacy

PixOrg is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, please let us know so we can delete it.

9. Security

The app's database and index are stored in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. Vault photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key held in the Android keystore and protected by your device credential. Because no user data is transmitted, there is no data in transit to secure. We still recommend keeping your phone's screen lock enabled.

When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and describe any new data flow clearly. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
